# TamaNaruto v1.0.0

The first Naruto release, built on the TamaPoke engine by way of TamaDigi.

- 33 ninja from Konoha, Suna, Kiri and Oto, drawn from the Naruto: Ninja Council sprites
- Every ninja keeps its rank from the series (Academy, Genin, Chunin, Jonin, Kage); nobody turns into anybody else
- First start: pick your village, then your Genin (Naruto, Sasuke or Sakura in Konoha)
- Missions ranked by your level; recruit defeated ninja with sealing scrolls
- A Chunin exam per village, with ninja from every village taking part
- Chakra natures (Katon, Futon, Raiton, Doton, Suiton, Mokuton, Yin, Yang) and the Taijutsu > Genjutsu > Ninjutsu style triangle, 74 jutsu
- Original music, seals, avatars and battle backgrounds
