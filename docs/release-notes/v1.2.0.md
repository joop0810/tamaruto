# TamaNaruto v1.2.0

- **Learning a new jutsu shows what you would learn.** The new jutsu now sits at the top as its own card, with its chakra nature and power, in the same format as the four you already know, and a line underneath says to tap the one it should replace. Before, it was only a coloured word above four full cards, so it looked as if the screen only offered to forget something.
- **Calmer fight backgrounds.** Fights now take place on open ground with just a low outline of the village on the horizon (the carved cliff, the dunes, the misty mountains, the forest edge, the arena stands). Your opponent no longer seems to be standing on the rooftops, and both fighters stand out more. The main screen keeps the full villages.

**What you need to do:** install the firmware. Your save stays: leave "Erase device" unticked. The village packs on the SD card do not change.
