# TamaNaruto v1.1.0

- **Every village has its own backdrop.** Konoha with the carved cliff and red roofs, Suna with its domed houses between the canyon walls, Kiri on stilts over misty water, Oto with its hideout in a dark forest, and an arena for the Chunin exam with the crowd on the stands. Your ninja stands in its own village, and a fight takes place in your opponent's village. At night the windows and lanterns light up.
- The old meadow, beach and snow backgrounds from the Digimon version are gone.

**What you need to do:** install the firmware. Your save stays: leave "Erase device" unticked. The village packs on the SD card do not change.
